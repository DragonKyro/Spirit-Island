# Spirit Island - Wikipedia Reference Summary

> Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirit_Island_(board_game)
> Fetched: 2026-05-04
> Purpose: Quick-reference notes for digital adaptation development.

## Game Overview
- **Type**: Cooperative euro-style strategy board game
- **Designer**: R. Eric Reuss
- **Publisher**: Greater Than Games
- **Released**: 2017
- **Players**: 1-4 (base); 1-6 with Jagged Earth
- **Theme**: Anti-colonial - players are island spirits driving off invaders

## Win / Loss Conditions

**Victory:**
- Clear all Invader settlements from the island
- Generate enough Fear to lower victory criteria (Terror Levels)

**Defeat (any triggers loss):**
- Too many Blight tokens accumulate (blight card depleted)
- A Spirit loses all Presence tokens
- Invader action deck is depleted on Explore

## Turn Structure

### Spirit Phase
- Growth (gain presence, power cards, energy)
- Energy gained
- Power cards played (paying energy cost)
- Fast Powers resolve

### Invader Phase
Driven by the Invader action deck:
- **Explore** - invaders expand into new lands
- **Build** - existing explorers/towns build into towns/cities
- **Ravage** - invaders attack dahan and add Blight to undefended lands

### Slow Powers / Time Passes
- Slow Powers resolve
- Cleanup, advance Invader cards

## Key Mechanics

- **Fear**: Generated by player actions; reaching thresholds reveals Fear cards and advances Terror Levels, easing victory conditions.
- **Blight**: Placed on lands during Ravage when damage isn't fully prevented. Excess blight cascades to adjacent lands and depletes the Blight card.
- **Presence**: Spirit's physical footprint on the island; fueling powers and triggering effects. Losing all of it = loss.
- **Dahan**: Native islanders who fight alongside spirits.
- **Terror Levels**: TL1 → TL2 → TL3, each with progressively easier victory conditions.

## Spirits (Total Count by Set)
- Base Game: 8
- Branch & Claw: 2
- Jagged Earth: 10
- Feather & Flame (promo packs): 4
- Nature Incarnate: 8

## Expansions (Chronological)
1. **Branch & Claw** - adds Events, Disease, Beasts, Wilds, Badlands
2. **Jagged Earth** - large expansion, adds 6-player support, new mechanics
3. **Feather & Flame** - promo content compilation
4. **Nature Incarnate** (2023) - funded via BackerKit

## Accessibility Variant
- **Horizons of Spirit Island** (2022) - simplified entry-level standalone with beginner-friendly spirits and lighter components.

## Reception
- BoardGameGeek: ranked #9-10 globally (2022-2025)
- #1 solo game on BGG People's Choice 2024 (third consecutive year)
- Notable as a flat-out anti-colonial cooperative game
- Known for high complexity and analysis paralysis risk
